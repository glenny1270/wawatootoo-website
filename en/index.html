A kids' art & drawing school, 1 minute from Kameido Station

Wawatootoo art studio is an art school in Koto City, Tokyo — one minute on foot from Kameido Station's East Exit. We offer a Kids' Art course for ages 3 through elementary school, and a Drawing course for ages 11 to adult, in small classes of 3–4 students.

Observe, feel, then draw. From a child's first painting to serious drawing training, all materials are provided by the studio. Looking for an art class around Kameido, Kinshicho or Ojima? Come and try a trial lesson.
