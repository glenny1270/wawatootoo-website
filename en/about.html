01 — Concept

Rethinking how we learn art, in the age of AI.

When images can be generated by AI in seconds, the goal of art education should no longer stop at technique and finished work.

How to observe the world, discover questions, form your own judgment, and turn ideas into a visual language of your own — that is what we teach.

At Wawatootoo art studio, every week is a new artistic exploration. Our kids' art course is built around original projects — each week a different theme, subject of observation, artistic idea and way of making.

We follow what is new in the art world and in diverse creative practices, continually renewing our curriculum — drawing inspiration from cultures, artists, artworks, natural phenomena and everyday life, and shaping this wealth of resources into projects children can understand and explore.

Learning art is more than acquiring a skill: it cultivates the ability to perceive the world, think independently, find and solve problems, and express oneself creatively.

We hope WATO is where a child's artistic exploration takes off. — WHERE ART TAKES OFF
