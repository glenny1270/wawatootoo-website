01 — Concept

It all starts with a single pencil.

A small atelier in Kameido, Tokyo. WATO teaches the art of seeing, through drawing and painting.

WATO is a small atelier in a quiet corner of Kameido. Here you'll find the sound of pencils, the smell of paint, and a calm kind of focus.

Drawing is the deepest foundation of art — and a training in how to look. Capturing form, reading light and shadow, rendering texture: this cycle builds real observational skill.

From a three-year-old's first brush to serious drawing training for adults — small classes, tailored to each student's stage and goal.

A single pencil can change the way you see the world.
